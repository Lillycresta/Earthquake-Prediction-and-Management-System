SAFETY GUIDANCE

Earthquake Management

Practical actions for before, during and after an earthquake.

01

Before an earthquake

  • Secure heavy furniture and hanging objects.
  • Prepare a household emergency kit.
  • Identify safe places in each room.
  • Review evacuation routes and emergency contacts.
02

During an earthquake

  • Drop, cover and hold on.
  • Stay away from windows and heavy objects.
  • Do not use elevators or rush outside.
  • If outdoors, move away from buildings and overhead hazards.
03

After an earthquake

  • Expect and prepare for aftershocks.
  • Check for injuries and hazards.
  • Use phones only for emergencies.
  • Follow official alerts and local authority instructions.
